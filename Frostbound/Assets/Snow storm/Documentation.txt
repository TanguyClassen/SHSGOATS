Snow storm

Currently package works only in HDRP.
Package includes 4 VFX graphs with different effects required to create beliviable blizzard or a snow storm,
it also includes a bench, lamp post and a ground mesh.

Bench triangle count and texture size: 8168, 2k
Lamp post triangle count and texture size: 2894, 2k
Ground mesh triangle count and texture size: 8325, 2k

Snow particles textures imported with resolution of 2048x2048, but lowered in unity to 256x256 for optimization.

Package also includes one example scene.
